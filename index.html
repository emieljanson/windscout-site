Catch the good wind.

I built Windscout because I kept checking the same forecast, hoping a good window would appear. It keeps the next five days visible at home, so you know when to make time and go.

Windscout is an always-on e-ink forecast for your spot. Average wind, gusts, direction and weather are there whenever you glance at it. No unlocking your phone, refreshing the forecast or opening another app.

  • See the week — compare five days at a glance
  • Know the window — wind, gusts, direction and weather together
  • Always on — no apps, notifications or scrolling
  • Months unplugged — designed for up to three months per charge
  • Place it anywhere — on a desk, shelf or wall
